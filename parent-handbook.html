Parent Handbook

This page contains important information for Countryside Elementary families.

School Hours

School begins at 8:00 AM and ends at 3:00 PM.

Attendance

Please contact the school office if your child will be absent or late.

Drop-Off and Pick-Up

Students should be dropped off and picked up in the designated areas near the front entrance.

Visitors

All visitors must check in at the main office before entering the school building.
